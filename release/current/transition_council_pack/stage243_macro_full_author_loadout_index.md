## 1. Ordered Discussion Flow

The conversation has been organized into the following sequence:

### Step 1 — Meaning of Claude Data

Claude SeqCard data is treated as a scene-function and intent map, not as text to imitate.

Core conclusion:

```text
Claude data tells GPT V1700 what a scene does inside a story.
It must not be used as raw text reproduction material.
```

### Step 2 — Claude 16 Scene Function Core

The 16 scene-function labels are retained as the core taxonomy:

```text
ESTABLISH, ORACLE, INTRO, BOND, CONFLICT, REVERSAL,
LOSS, PUNISH, REVELATION, REUNION, RELIEF, ROMANCE,
PERIL, RESCUE, DESIRE, HOOK
```

### Step 3 — Why 16 Labels Are Not Enough

The 16 labels answer:

```text
What function does this scene perform?
```

They do not fully answer:

```text
Where does this scene belong in the season?
What caused it?
What does it cause?
What does it plant or pay off?
Whose belief or relationship changes?
Can the scene be removed?
Does a generated draft satisfy the intended function?
```

### Step 4 — GPT V1700 Macro Analysis Expansion

GPT V1700 adds the following layers above the 16 core functions:

```text
Layer 0. Identity
```
